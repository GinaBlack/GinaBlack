# 💫 About Me:
I'm a graduate from the University of Buea, currently working as a Software Engineer at a 3D Printing High-Tech Center in Yaoundé, where I build the software that keeps the center's services — bookings, projects, trainings — running online.<br><br>Outside of work, I build systems that sit at the intersection of AI and real-world infrastructure gaps in Cameroon: turning CT scans into 3D-printable assistive devices, teaching cameras to spot road accidents in real time, and reimagining what a home address can look like without a street name.<br><br>I'm aiming next for a Master's in Artificial Intelligence, with a focus on AI for healthcare — especially medical image analysis.<br><br>🔭 What I'm Building<br>🩻 MeMo3D: an AI-integrated web platform that segments CT scans (MONAI 2D U-Net) and reconstructs them into printable 3D assistive devices (Marching Cubes + parametric modeling), proven on a wrist-splint prototype.<br>🚦 UTWatch: a real-time traffic monitoring and accident detection system fusing YOLOv11n object detection, a ConvLSTM video classifier, and an audio crash-sound model with perfect accident recall on test data, ~2.5s end-to-end latency on a standard CPU.<br>📍 PinPoint: a GPS-based digital addressing platform for Cameroon, replacing landmark-based delivery with anonymous/alias links, real-time chat, and a bilingual EN/FR interface.<br>🎯 A facial-recognition + geo-fencing attendance system for smart, real-time identity verification.<br><br><br>🌍 A Bit More<br>🗣️ English (fluent) · Français (intermédiaire)<br>💬 Ask me about medical image segmentation, computer vision pipelines, or building tech for low-resource environments<br>
